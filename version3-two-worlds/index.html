Behind the Lens

Photography found me before code did. Growing up in Nigeria, I started capturing moments with whatever I could get my hands on — first a phone camera, then a friend's DSLR, eventually my own gear.

Now based in the Netherlands, I shoot the interplay between light and shadow on Amsterdam's canals, the raw energy of street life, and the quiet beauty of golden hour portraits. Every image is a single frame of a much longer story.

As an engineer, I think in systems and structures. As a photographer, I think in light and feeling. The tension between those two minds is where my best work lives.
